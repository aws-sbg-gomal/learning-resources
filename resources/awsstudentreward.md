## What Is It?

**AWS Student Rewards** is a program inside [AWS Builder Center](https://builder.aws.com) that gives verified university students up to **$579 in free AWS benefits**. You earn them by learning, building things, and staying active in the community. No catches, no paid subscription required to get started.

## Who Is Eligible?

- You need to be enrolled at a **higher education institution** (university or college)
- You must be **18 or older**
- Student status is verified through **SheerID**, so no manual paperwork is needed in most cases

## How to Claim Your Rewards, Step by Step

### Step 1: Create or sign in to your AWS account

Head to [https://aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com) and sign in. If you do not have an account yet, creating one is free and takes a few minutes.

### Step 2: Join AWS Builder Center

Go to [https://builder.aws.com](https://builder.aws.com) and sign in with your AWS account. Fill out your profile with your name, bio, and areas of interest. This is also where all your rewards will show up.

### Step 3: Verify your student status through SheerID

1. Find the **Student Rewards** banner on your Builder Center dashboard and click it.
2. Click **"Verify my student status"** to start the SheerID flow.
3. Enter your **full name**, **date of birth**, **university name**, and **university email address**.
4. SheerID tries to verify you instantly. If that does not work, it will ask you to upload a supporting document like an enrollment letter, student ID, or transcript.
5. Check your inbox for the result. It usually comes through within minutes, though sometimes it takes a few hours.

SheerID is a third-party verification service used by hundreds of companies. It only uses your info to confirm eligibility. You can read more about how it works at [https://www.sheerid.com](https://www.sheerid.com).

### Step 4: Get your Skill Builder Premium access

Once your student status is verified and your profile is complete, **12 months of AWS Skill Builder Premium** (worth $449) activates automatically. Log in and start using it at [https://skillbuilder.aws](https://skillbuilder.aws).

### Step 5: Earn badges to unlock credits and your exam voucher

Keep showing up on AWS Builder Center to collect badges. Here is what each milestone unlocks:

| Milestone | What You Get |
|-----------|--------------|
| 7 badges | $10 in AWS credits |
| 14 badges | Another $20 in AWS credits ($30 total) |
| 21 badges | $100 AWS Foundational Certification exam voucher |

Ways to earn badges:
- Publish articles or technical write-ups
- Comment on and engage with posts from other builders
- Complete learning activities and platform challenges
- Share projects you have been working on

### Step 6: Redeem your AWS credits

Credits are added directly to your AWS account. After you hit the badge milestones, they show up in your [AWS Billing Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/billing/home#/credits) within a few days. They apply automatically to your usage.

### Step 7: Use your certification exam voucher

Once you have earned 21 badges and received the voucher code:
1. Go to [https://www.aws.training/certification](https://www.aws.training/certification)
2. Pick an exam date and location that works for you.
3. Enter the voucher code at checkout to cover the cost.

The voucher works for any **AWS Foundational level exam**, including Cloud Practitioner and AI Practitioner.

## Tips to Get the Most Out of It

**Start as soon as you verify.** Your Skill Builder Premium runs for 12 months from the day it activates, not from when you enrolled in school. Every week you wait is a week of access you lose.

**Write about what you are learning.** Publishing even short articles on Builder Center is one of the faster ways to stack up badges. You do not need to be an expert to contribute.

**Go for Cloud Practitioner first.** It is the most approachable AWS cert and sets a solid base for everything else. Your voucher covers it completely.

**Layer it with AWS Free Tier.** The $30 in credits you earn come on top of the [AWS Free Tier](https://aws.amazon.com/free/), which already gives you 12 months of free usage across 60+ services. You have more runway than you might think.

**Use the hands-on labs in Skill Builder.** They spin up real AWS environments at no cost to you, so you can practice without touching your credits.
